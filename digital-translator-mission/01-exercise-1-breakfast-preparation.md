# Exercise 1: Breakfast Preparation

## 1. Understanding the Task

The task is to prepare breakfast consisting of:
- Two fried eggs.
- Three slices of toasted bread.
- A cup or small jug of chocolate and Milo drink.

## 2. Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 3 slices of bread
- Groundnut oil
- 1 sachet of milk
- 1 sachet of chocolate
- 1 sachet of Milo
- 2 teaspoons of sugar
- 2 cups of water

## 3. Equipment

- Electric kettle
- Stove
- Frying pan
- Electric toaster
- Plate
- Cup or small jug large enough for the drink
- Spoon
- Spatula

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Go to the kitchen.
2. Gather all the required ingredients and equipment.
3. Pour two cups of water into the electric kettle.
4. Switch on the kettle and wait until the water boils.
5. Put the milk, chocolate, Milo and sugar into a cup or suitable jug.
6. Carefully pour in the hot water and stir until everything dissolves.
7. Put the frying pan on the stove.
8. Add a suitable amount of groundnut oil to the pan.
9. Crack the two eggs into the pan.
10. Fry the eggs and turn them over carefully.
11. Continue frying until the egg whites are fully cooked and the yolks are firm.
12. Put the three slices of bread into the electric toaster.
13. Toast the bread until it is properly toasted.
14. Remove the eggs and bread carefully and place them on a plate.
15. Serve the drink with the fried eggs and toasted bread.

## 5. Assumptions and Missing Details

- The kitchen has electricity or another available power source.
- The kettle, stove and toaster are working.
- The ingredients are available.
- The frying pan and other equipment are clean.
- The person preparing the breakfast knows how to operate the equipment safely.

## 6. Expected Result

Breakfast is successfully prepared when:
- Two fully cooked fried eggs are ready.
- Three slices of bread are properly toasted.
- The chocolate and Milo drink is ready.
- All the food and drink are properly served.

## 7. Lesson Learned

Computers need clear and precise instructions. If an instruction is incomplete or unclear, the task may not produce the expected result.
