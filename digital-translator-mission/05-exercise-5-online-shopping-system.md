# Exercise 5: Online Shopping System

## 1. Understanding the Task

The task is to explain how an online shopping system works, from the moment a customer places an order until the system displays the result.

## 2. Customer Scenario

A customer wants to buy two phones from an online store.

- Product: Phone
- Quantity: 2
- Price per phone: ₦150,000
- Delivery location: Lagos
- Payment method: Bank transfer

## 3. INPUT

The system receives the following information from the customer:

- Product selected
- Quantity required
- Delivery location
- Payment method
- Customer and delivery details

## 4. PROGRAM / INSTRUCTIONS

The online shopping system follows instructions to:

1. Receive the customer's order.
2. Check whether the selected product is available.
3. Calculate the total price.
4. Record the order details.
5. Process or verify the payment.
6. Arrange delivery after the required checks are successful.

## 5. PROCESSING

The system calculates the total cost:

Quantity × Price per phone

2 × ₦150,000 = ₦300,000

Therefore, the total product cost is **₦300,000**, excluding any delivery charges.

The system also checks the product availability and payment status.

## 6. STORAGE

The system stores relevant information, such as:

- Customer details
- Product ordered
- Quantity
- Total cost
- Delivery location
- Payment method
- Order status

The information can be retrieved later to track the order.

## 7. OUTPUT

The system displays the order summary:

- Product: Phone
- Quantity: 2
- Total cost: ₦300,000
- Delivery location: Lagos
- Payment method: Bank transfer
- Payment status: Pending verification

The system can display an order confirmation while waiting for payment verification.

## 8. Expected Result

The customer receives an order summary. Once payment is successfully verified and the order is confirmed, the store can proceed with delivery.

## 9. Lesson Learned

An online shopping system receives customer information as input, follows program instructions, processes the order, stores important details and produces an output for the customer.
