# Final Reflection: The Digital Translator

## How Does a Computer Work With Information and Instructions?

A computer does not understand plain English like humans. Therefore, it needs data and instructions to know what information to work with, what to do, and how to perform a task.

Data provides the information the computer needs, while instructions tell the computer how to process that information and produce a result.

For example, when we want a computer to calculate the cost of two phones, we provide the price and quantity as data. The computer follows instructions to multiply the price by the quantity and display the total cost.

This shows that computers depend on data and instructions to perform tasks.
